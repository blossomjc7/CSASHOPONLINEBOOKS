About the Founder & Series

🌟 Our Safe Space Mission

This collection is designed to cultivate emotional regulation, perspective, self-talk, environmental awareness, and digital balance for kids. Rather than banning digital devices, we believe in engineering premium, ad-free educational sanctuaries with absolutely no stranger interactions or inappropriate content.

Privacy Policy

Your Child's Privacy is Sacred.

Our application is built specifically to safeguard young minds. We collect ZERO personal information, utilize no background tracking protocols, display absolutely no external advertisements, and maintain no backend databases storing minor records.

All settings (such as your reading progress, Kiswahili word count, selected language, and speaker voice character) are saved 100% locally on your own personal device via browser LocalStorage.

We guarantee a clean, isolated, and extremely safe reading sanctuary.

🍪

Cookie Policy

Simple, Non-Tracking Local Storage.

We only use essential local records (browser local storage) to ensure the system functions smoothly for children.

These local records are limited to:

  • Saving your current page index for each book.
  • Remembering your selected voice setting and reading language.
  • Saving whether you chose to accept or decline our cookie consent banner.

We do not use advertising, behavioral, or analytical cookies that track minors across the internet.

Let's Read Safely!
